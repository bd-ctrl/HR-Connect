# Assessment card mascot

Asset: `assessment-peek.webp`, 600 × 800, transparent alpha. Created with the built-in image generator, using the existing `rating-4.png` as the character/style reference. The private placement screenshot is not included in the repository. The optimized WebP is used by the employee assessment form; the generated original is retained separately.

Desktop: large character outside the card's right edge, with two hands holding the white border. Phone/tablet: decorative illustration in the person header, without covering fields. Validation, storage and permissions are unchanged by the decoration.

Prompt brief: One friendly yellow five-point star, cheerful crescent eyes and open smile, peach cheeks, coral buttoned suit and white shirt, soft colored-pencil texture matching the reference. Peek from the right of an invisible vertical edge near the left of the asset. Both yellow hands grip that edge at different heights. Large head and upper torso, all visible contours inside the canvas. True transparent background. No screenshot, text, green frame, rocket, speech bubbles, wall or extra props.
